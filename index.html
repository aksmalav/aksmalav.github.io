Clearing a 40,000 task backlog for a health system in three days

Luminai, sole product manager, 2026

40K+ backlog cleared in 3 days, $110K+ in annual savings.

Problem
A health system's front-line staff were buried under a growing queue of notifications that each needed manual handling, pulling time away from patient-facing work.
What I did
Ran user interviews with the staff doing the work, scoped two AI notification-clearing automations around what they actually needed, and pitched the client CIO to secure buy-in before building.
Shipped
Led both automations from design through go-live, defining launch KPIs and success criteria up front.
Bigger picture
These sat inside a portfolio of 17 automations I owned at the account, which held 99% uptime and drove $3.8M in annual impact (6x ROI). I also rebuilt a legacy workflow that was mismatching patient data via direct API integration, raising throughput 500%; that approach was later extended to Cleveland Clinic.

Building the company's first engineering capacity dashboard myself

Luminai, built with Claude Code, 2026

Better allocation decisions across 126+ workflows, ~$60K in annual labor savings.

Problem
More than 20 engineers across time zones were split across 126+ concurrent workflows, with no single view of who had capacity and where work was piling up.
What I did
Designed a capacity-scoring model and built the dashboard tool myself using Claude Code, alongside the company's first workflow registry.
Shipped
Used it to run weekly allocation syncs with the global engineering team, turning staffing from guesswork into a shared, data-backed conversation.
